The lock covers WRM and asks for your password (or a PIN, if you set one); terminals, transfers, tunnels and AI sessions keep running.

- **Lock now:** 🔒 in the top bar or **Ctrl+Alt+L**.
- **Automatically** (*Settings → Security → Screen lock*): after inactivity (with an optional warning), when WRM is hidden, when the computer locks or sleeps, or at every start.
- **Lock screen:** background, clock, your name, how many windows keep running and a message of your own. Notifications wait until you unlock.

Administrators can set a maximum idle time for everybody.
