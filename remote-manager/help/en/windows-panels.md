## Windows
Every opened connection is a window with a tab. Drag the title bar to move it, double-click to maximise, drag it to a screen edge to **snap** (or use the snap button for layouts). **+ New Window** (Alt+N) opens an empty window with your connections and saved sessions. Right-click a title bar for *Pin in place* and *Always on top*.

## Panels
Connections, Sessions, Snippets, Clipboard, AI sessions and docked addons are **panels**. **▦ Panels** in the top bar shows or hides them. Drag a panel by its tab: onto another tab bar to stack them, onto the top or bottom of a panel to split, to a screen edge to dock, or onto the canvas to float. **Lock layout** stops accidental moves; **Reset** brings the default back. The layout is stored with your account.

## Workboard
The window area is one canvas. **▤ Workboard → ＋ Add addon** places small tools on it (server metrics, notes, clocks, checks…); each is a window you can move, resize, dock as a panel or remove. Only what you add is shown.

## Sessions
**+ Save Session** in the Sessions panel saves all open windows with their places; click a session to open it again (*Replace* or *Add*). Right-click to update, rename, lock or delete it.

## Clipboard
The Clipboard panel keeps the last 20 texts copied from terminals in this browser; click to copy again, **Paste** to send to the focused terminal. Signing out clears it.
