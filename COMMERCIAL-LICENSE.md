This page explains in plain language what that means. The [LICENSE](LICENSE) file is the legally
binding text, including the licensor's clarifications at its top. If anything here differs from it, the LICENSE wins.

**for any noncommercial purpose**, for example:

- personal use, home labs, hobby projects, learning and experimenting
- non-profit and charitable organizations

When you share the software or a fork, you must include the [LICENSE](LICENSE) and keep its

## ✅ Free for companies: use WRM as a work tool

Companies and other organizations of any size may use WRM **for their own work, free of charge**:

- install and run it on your own servers, PCs or cloud accounts, for any number of users
- let your employees and contractors use it to manage your servers
- use it for **paid work for your own customers**, for example an IT service provider or freelancer
  managing customers' servers, as long as customers pay for **your work** and not for WRM
- invite customers or partners into a shared session or voice call while you work with them
- adapt and modify it for your own needs

You may not hand out copies to others under this license. Everyone who wants to run WRM gets it
from the [official releases](https://github.com/vedranius/web-browser-RDM-public/releases).

## 💼 Needs a commercial license

Contact the owner and agree on a license (and fee) **before** you:

- offer WRM, original or modified, to others as a **hosted, managed or cloud service / SaaS**, or give
  third parties their own accounts on a WRM installation that you run for them
- **charge anyone for access to or use of WRM**, or include access to WRM in a paid product, plan,
  subscription or hosting package
- **sell, resell, rent, sublicense or white-label** WRM
- **bundle** WRM with, or include WRM or its code in, a product, appliance or service you distribute
  or offer to others
- distribute WRM or modified versions of it for a commercial purpose

### Examples

| Situation | Free? |
|---|---|
| A company's IT department uses WRM to manage the company's servers | ✅ Free |
| An IT service provider's technicians use WRM to manage customers' servers; customers are billed for the work | ✅ Free |
| A freelancer uses WRM for paid projects and invites the client into a shared session | ✅ Free |
| A company modifies WRM for internal use and runs it for all its offices and subsidiaries | ✅ Free |
| A hosting provider offers WRM to its customers as a control panel or add-on | 💼 Commercial license |
| Someone runs WRM and sells subscriptions or accounts to it | 💼 Commercial license |
| A company sells servers, appliances or software with WRM included | 💼 Commercial license |
| A company offers a product built on WRM's code | 💼 Commercial license |

Not sure whether your case is covered? Ask before you start. It is quick, and the answer is usually simple.
